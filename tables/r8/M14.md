# Milestone 14: table selector on licence-allowed data, PP-LCNet gate, scanned-table fixtures

Run **45723794511** (workflow `tables-m14`, job `sel`): `gate14.py` (PP-LCNet gate from the round-8 per-table files) and `sel14.py` (selector retrained on the round-8 cells and words of run 45676430603). The first milestone-14 run (45711587938) trained on every set, including evaluation-only ones; its selector is withdrawn. The synthetic scanned-table fixtures are still those of run 45711587938 (`gen14.py`, job `fixtures`).

## Summary

- **Licences.** Only sets whose own licence allows commercial use of derived models train the shipped selector (table below). WTW (CC BY-NC 4.0), cTDaR (no licence) and ICDAR-2013 (no licence) are evaluated on, never trained on, never used to choose a variant or a threshold, and none of their rows or ids are in the harness repository.
- **Shipped: the licence-clean selector `w0`**: 300 trees over the same 56 features, trained on 10,212 unrotated tables of r4pt, r4scitsr, pt2, ftnc, r4brgm and tabrec. PubTabNet (answer-key words, optimistic) and SynFinTabs (synthetic) are left out: the variant was chosen by the rule fixed in advance (best leave-one-dataset-out TEDS-Struct on the dev split of the allowed real-PDF-word sets: weight 1 .9463, 0.5 .9467, 0.25 .9479, 0 .9492).
- **Real PDFs, test split** (r4pt, r4scitsr, r4brgm; 4,306 tables), TEDS-Struct / exact shape: TableFormer .9262 / 81.4%, TATR .9546 / 81.4%, round-5 selector on main .9670 / 84.8% (in-sample: its trees saw these tables, and ICDAR-2013), **new selector .9630 / 84.4% (grouped CV), .9551 / 83.6% (leave one dataset out)**. The new selector beats TableFormer alone (+3.68 points [+3.34, +4.08], +3.1 pp shape) and TATR alone (+0.84 [+0.45, +1.37], +3.0 pp shape) under grouped CV; on unseen sources it ties TATR on TEDS-Struct (+0.05 [−0.25, +0.42]) and still beats both on exact shape (+2.2 pp). Adding pt2 and ftnc (real PDF words on image pages, 6,469 tables): .9582 / 81.3% (CV), .9522 / 80.6% (LODO) against TableFormer .9163 / 80.5% and TATR .9510 / 78.1%; exact shape against TableFormer is +0.8 pp [+0.1, +1.5] (CV) and +0.1 [−0.8, +0.9] (LODO), a tie. So the selector stays the default.
- **Against the round-5 selector on main:** lower on the r4 sets (−0.40 points CV), where round 5 is in-sample; on sets round 5 never saw (the new selector by grouped CV on pt2, ftnc and TabRecSet; never trained on PubTabNet or the evaluation-only sets) it is level or better: pt2 .9412 vs .9394, ftnc .9547 vs .9538, PubTabNet .9488 vs .9488 (89.5% vs 89.1% shape), TabRecSet .8296 vs .8008, evaluation-only sets .6652 vs .6578. The round-5 trees saw ICDAR-2013 in training, so they are not licence-clean either.
- **Where TableFormer alone is better:** synthetic financial tables (SynFinTabs: TableFormer .9114 / 59.0%, new selector .8271 / 22.4%, round 5 .8585 / 35.2%) and exact shape on FinTabNet.c (TableFormer 83.9%, selector 81.1%, round 5 81.4%; TEDS-Struct .9547 vs .9057). The type check "exact shape within 2 points of TableFormer" fails on financial tables for every selector (new 53.9%, round 5 60.0%, TableFormer 72.4%; SynFinTabs dominates the type). Training on SynFinTabs fixes it only in-domain (weight 1, CV 59.4%; left out of training 30.3%), so it is a synthetic-domain effect, not a real-PDF one.
- **PP-LCNet gate: 0.75** (was 0.8), now chosen on the allowed sets only (gate14.py; rule: lowest gate with no average loss on upright scanned crops and at most 0.5% of upright docling crops turned). Test split: TabRecSet upright crops +0.00 points (0.4% turned), turned crops +23.6 points (92.9% fixed); evaluation-only scans upright +0.28, turned +13.6 (76.7% fixed); 0.3% of upright docling crops of the allowed sets turned. The earlier 0.8 had been chosen on dev data that included WTW and cTDaR.
- **A PP-LCNet turn forces TATR, measured on docling crops of scans** (gate 0.75): against the selector on the crop as is, forced TATR −0.04 points [−0.19, +0.10] (test, 1,371 scan tables, 33 turned) and −0.11 [−0.29, +0.05] (dev); letting the selector decide on the turned crop instead +0.01 [−0.02, +0.05] over forced (test), +0.06 [−0.06, +0.20] (dev); exact shape equal. No measurable harm from forcing, and the selector was never trained on TableFormer's output for a sideways crop (turned tables are left out of training), while TATR on the turned crop is what fixes truly turned scans (+23.6 points on turned TabRecSet crops). Kept: forced TATR.
- **Fixtures for the harness:** `selector_fixture.json` (80 training rows: r4scitsr 24, r4pt 19, ftnc 15, pt2 13, tabrec 6, r4brgm 3) and `features_fixture.json` (12 tables of r4pt, pt2 and ftnc with the 56 features) are this run's outputs unchanged. `tatr_fixtures.json` (round 5, `r5_tatr.py`) lost its three ICDAR-2013 cases through `fixclean14.py`.
