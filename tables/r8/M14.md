# Milestone 14: selector retrained on the round-8 tables, scanned-table fixtures

Run **45711587938** (workflow `tables-m14`): job `sel` (`sel14.py` on the round-8 cells and words of run 45676430603) and job `fixtures` (`gen14.py`, synthetic scanned pages for the harness tests). Exported trees, selector and feature fixtures and the scan/PP-LCNet fixtures went to the harness (branch `m14`).

- **Adopted: the retrained selector with the same 56 features.** It passes the gate: overall above the committed selector (+0.82 TEDS-Struct points, +2.0 pp exact shape), and exact shape within 2 points of TableFormer on every type (financial −1.04).
- **Not adopted: the extra table-level features.** They did not help on the dev split, and on test they add +0.01 points (p = 0.67).
- **PP-LCNet gate for scans: 0.8,** chosen on the dev split from the round-8 per-table files. On the test split it fixes 80% of turned scans (+16.8 points) and turns 1.9% of upright scans (+0.2 points).
- **Scanned-table fixtures:** synthetic 7 × 4 ruled table, upright and turned 90°. PP-LCNet reads 0 and 270 at confidence 0.92; the round-8 OCR arm reads all 27 lines and TATR gives the 7 × 4 grid on both.
